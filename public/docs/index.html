How Elitechnexus works

A plain-language guide to jobs, paid projects, and proof-first placement — for talent and for companies hiring into US tech teams.

What we help with

Elitechnexus LLC connects global engineers with full-time roles, paid project work, and interview prep. Employers get vetted candidates with evidence before they commit.

Full-time jobs

Long-term placement into US-facing teams. We align on skills, seniority, timezone overlap, and communication before introductions.

Paid projects

Scoped engagements for product builds, migrations, and surge capacity — with clear deliverables and review loops.

Interview coaching

Live coding, system design, and behavioral prep so candidates show up ready and employers see signal early.

For candidates

  1. Apply — Share your background, target roles, and availability through Get started / Login.
  2. Prove — Complete technical vetting: live coding, architecture, and screening so your skills are documented.
  3. Interview — We coach and coordinate introductions with hiring teams that match your profile.
  4. Place — Accept an offer or project. We stay in the loop through onboarding and early weeks.

For companies

  1. Scope — Tell us the role or project: stack, seniority, outcomes, and communication needs.
  2. Review proof — See vetted candidates with evidence from technical screening — not résumé-only guesses.
  3. Interview & hire — Run your process with our coordination and interview support.
  4. Onboard — We help with compliant onboarding and keep both sides aligned after start.

Proof first — what “vetted” means

Live coding

Hands-on problem solving under realistic constraints — not trivia quizzes alone.

Architecture

System design discussions so senior and mid-level talent can show judgment at scale.

Screening signal

Structured notes employers can review before calendars fill up with low-fit calls.

What to expect

Timelines vary by role and market. Most placements move faster when scope is clear and proof is already complete.
