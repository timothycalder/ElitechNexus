1. Security Policy

Elitechnexus LLC (“Elitechnexus,” “we,” “us”) helps people and companies turn skills into full-time jobs, paid projects, and lasting income through proof-first placement. As part of that work, we may receive and process business-sensitive information — including personal data, professional profiles, hiring requirements, interview notes, and related communications. Protecting that information is essential to our clients’ interests, to candidates’ trust, and to our own operations.

This Security Policy explains our commitment to protect information and assets we own or are responsible for, and to maintain an efficient, safe, and secure working environment for Elitechnexus, our customers, partners, and the talent we support.

1.1 Purpose and scope

The purpose of this Policy is to set clear expectations for how Elitechnexus safeguards information across our website, internal tools, vendor systems, and day-to-day placement workflows. It covers:

  • Candidate and client personal and professional information
  • Company hiring briefs, project scopes, and related business records
  • Account credentials, communications, and operational systems we use to deliver services
  • Devices, accounts, and access rights used by associates and authorized third parties

This Policy applies to Elitechnexus LLC operations and to associates, contractors, business partners, and external parties who have access to our infrastructure, IT systems, or information resources.

1.2 Security commitments

Elitechnexus will apply a practical, layered security approach intended to ensure:

  • Protection of information and assets against known and emerging threats through physical, logical, personnel, and information-security controls (including cyber security)
  • Responsible technology use, including AI-assisted tools where used in operations, with safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the data involved
  • Compliance with applicable legal and regulatory requirements in the markets where we operate
  • Business continuity so core placement and communication workflows can continue or recover in line with stakeholder obligations
  • Clear ownership of security responsibilities for teams and individuals who handle candidate, client, or company data
  • Awareness and competence so people with access understand how to handle information safely
  • Reporting channels for associates and stakeholders to report security weaknesses, violations, suspected incidents, or service disruption
  • Identify → Protect → Detect → Respond → Recover practices for security weaknesses and incidents
  • Continuous improvement through periodic review of security performance against practical targets
  • Third-party / supply-chain risk management for vendors and partners with access to our systems or information (including their personnel, where relevant)

1.3 How we protect information in practice

  • Access control: Access to candidate and client records is limited to people who need it for their role. Accounts should use strong authentication where available.
  • Least privilege: Permissions are granted based on job need and reviewed when roles change or engagements end.
  • Secure communications: Prefer approved channels for sharing résumés, screening notes, and hiring briefs. Avoid sending sensitive credentials through unsecured messages.
  • Vendor diligence: Tools used for email, scheduling, hosting, forms, or operations are selected with confidentiality and availability in mind.
  • Data handling: Personal and hiring data should not be copied to personal devices or shared outside approved workflows without authorization.
  • Incident readiness: Suspected breaches, lost devices, phishing, or unauthorized access should be reported promptly so we can contain and remediate.

1.4 Security Management System

This Policy is supported by internal standards, procedures, and guidelines that form our Security Management practices. Relevant expectations are shared with stakeholders who help implement them. Security ownership sits with Elitechnexus leadership (including designated security and operations leads). Practices are reviewed periodically so they remain relevant to our operations and to evolving stakeholder expectations.

1.5 Reporting and consequences

If you believe you have found a security weakness, or if you suspect unauthorized access to Elitechnexus systems or data, contact us immediately using the details in Section 4 and put “Security” in the subject line. Good-faith reports help us protect everyone who trusts us with information.

Violations of this Policy — including unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse of information — may result in removal of access, contract consequences, and other remedies permitted by law and by applicable agreements. Consequences apply equally to associates and to external parties with access rights.
